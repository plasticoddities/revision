Course Toolkit

The material that crosses the whole HSC course.

Concepts

Fundamental: persons, society, culture, environment, time.

Additional: power, authority, gender, identity, technologies, globalisation.

Research Methods

Case study, focus group, observation, personal reflection, secondary research, content analysis, interview, participant observation, questionnaire and statistical analysis.

Micro / Meso / Macro

Micro: individuals and close interactions. Meso: groups, organisations and institutions. Macro: large-scale social and cultural structures.

Social & Cultural Literacy

Use course concepts to interpret social and cultural life, recognise different perspectives, and connect personal experience with public knowledge.

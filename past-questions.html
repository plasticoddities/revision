Use past papers effectively

1. Identify the course area

Is the question testing Continuity & Change, Popular Culture, or Beliefs & Ideologies?

2. Identify the directive

Explain, analyse, assess, evaluate and justify require different levels of reasoning. Do not simply reproduce memorised content.

3. Break down every component

NESA feedback repeatedly identifies failure to address all components of a question as a weakness.

4. Build with concepts and evidence

Use course terminology and relevant, specific examples to support the response.

How to Answer

Built around recurring advice in NESA Society & Culture HSC marking feedback.

The recurring NESA message: answer the actual question, use Society & Culture concepts and terminology, support ideas with relevant examples, and move beyond description.

A simple response check

1

DIRECTIVE

What level of thinking is required?

2

CONTENT

What syllabus knowledge must be used?

3

CONCEPTS

Which concepts help explain the issue?

4

JUDGEMENT

What conclusion does the evidence support?

Explain

Show how or why. Establish clear relationships rather than simply identifying features.

Analyse

Break the issue into relevant components and show relationships, implications or consequences using course concepts.

Assess

Make and sustain a judgement about significance, impact, value or extent. Support that judgement with evidence.

Evaluate

Make a reasoned judgement using appropriate criteria and evidence. Consider strengths, limitations, consequences or effectiveness where relevant.

Extended responses

Plan first. Establish an argument that directly answers the question. Integrate concepts and specific examples throughout. Keep returning to the judgement rather than writing a pre-prepared case-study narrative.

Guidance is paraphrased from NESA marking feedback; it is not a replacement for the official marking guidelines.

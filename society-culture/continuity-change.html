Continuity & Change

Revise the concepts, theories and evidence used in the South Korea focus study.

1. Nature of continuity and change

Continuity refers to aspects of society and culture that remain relatively stable over time. Change involves alteration in social or cultural patterns. Change may be gradual/evolutionary or more substantial/transformative, and it can be resisted.

Think: What changes? What remains? Who benefits? Who may be disadvantaged?
2. Micro / Meso / Macro

Micro: individuals and close personal relationships. Meso: groups, organisations and institutions. Macro: large-scale social structures and society-wide patterns.

3. Modernisation, Westernisation and globalisation

Modernisation concerns social/economic development associated with industrialisation, urbanisation and technological change. Westernisation concerns Western values, ideas and lifestyles. Globalisation increases connections and flows between societies.

Class evidence: South Korean beauty culture and cosmetic surgery were used to consider Westernisation alongside continuity in social pressure, conformity and gender norms.

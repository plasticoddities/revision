Course Toolkit

Core concepts and research skills that can be applied throughout the HSC course.

Fundamental concepts

Persons · Society · Culture · Environment · Time

Additional concepts

Power · Authority · Gender · Identity · Technologies · Globalisation

Micro / Meso / Macro

Micro — individual and close interpersonal level.
Meso — groups, organisations and institutions.
Macro — broad social structures and society-wide patterns.

Research methods

Questionnaire · content analysis · statistical analysis · observation · interview · focus group · secondary research · personal reflection · participant observation · case study.

Research ethics

Informed consent; avoid harm/discomfort; openness; confidentiality/privacy; care with vulnerable groups; avoid covert research; recognise assumptions/bias; acknowledge sources.

Social & cultural literacy

Connect personal experience and public knowledge, use concepts accurately and move between micro, meso and macro.

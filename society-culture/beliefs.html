DEPTH STUDY · ISLAM

Beliefs & Ideologies

Revise the Society & Culture concepts through Islam as the class case study.

Society & Culture focus: this is not a Religious Studies treatment of Islam. Focus on how belief systems and ideologies influence individuals, groups and societies.
IslamWorldviewIdentityCommunityAuthoritySecularisation
1. Foundations

Revise belief system, ideology, worldview, identity, values and secularisation.

2. Identity and belonging

Distinguish personal and collective identity and recognise multiple identities.

Islam: the Ummah is the worldwide Muslim community and is useful evidence for collective identity and belonging.
3. How beliefs are expressed

Revise ritual, symbol, tradition, custom and language. Explain how practices communicate values or reinforce identity.

Examples: Salah, Ramadan, Hajj and Zakat; Allah, Ummah, mosque, Qur'an and Imam.
4. Community

Participation strengthens belonging and identity. Apply community and the Ummah at micro, meso and macro levels.

5. Authority and power

Distinguish authority from power. Institutions and accepted authority can maintain traditions and influence behaviour.

Islam: sacred texts, religious leaders, traditions and community expectations; the Qur'an is an important source of authority.
6. Continuity and change

Influences include technology, globalisation, migration, changing values and new generations. Reform responds to new circumstances; dissent may contribute to change.

Key judgement: core beliefs can remain while practices adapt.
7. Contemporary society and future

Revise acceptance/rejection, representation, prejudice/discrimination, secularisation, diversity/multiculturalism, technology and future directions.
